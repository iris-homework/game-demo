Cozy Story SFX — 50 free sounds for visual novels and dialogue scenes

Text blips (letter by letter), choices and menus, save and load, page turns, phone, doors,
a school chime, reactions (heartbeat, blush, realization, surprise, shock, sad sting,
comedy boing, sweat drop) and scene transitions. Soft and warm, made to sit under text.

Format: WAV, 44.1 kHz, 16-bit, mono, normalized; each sound starts and ends in silence.

How they were made: synthesized by our own code from filtered noise, resonators, a modeled
acoustic piano, music box, bells, marimba and pizzicato. No recorded samples.

Music for the same scenes: Cozy Story Music, 8 seamless loops (everyday life, heartwarming,
sad memories, comedy, mystery, first crush, rainy thoughts, happy ending), each with a calm
version (aquumgifts.itch.io).
